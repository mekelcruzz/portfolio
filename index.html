About Me

    

      I graduated from Batangas State University – The National Engineering University with a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology, majoring in Business Analytics. I develop responsive websites and applications using HTML, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript, React, Python, PostgreSQL, and SQLite. During my internship at BatStateU – The NEU Alangilan Campus Library, I worked with the KOHA Integrated Library System and developed a student attendance application for the university's extension campus libraries. I also completed the Amazon Web Services (AWS) Academy Cloud Foundations and Cloud Architecting programs, earning industry-recognized badges. I am passionate about building clean, responsive, and user-friendly digital solutions that combine modern web technologies with practical business needs.
